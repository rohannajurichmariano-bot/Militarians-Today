New SHS, New Hope: How the Strengthened Curriculum is Game-Changing for Us Grade 11s

Written & Photo Curated by: Zeresh May T. Casauran | Media Specialist

Introduction

Now as we enter grade 11, we have reached a point where all the choices that we make academically will start to influence our adult lives. High school can be a lot sometimes with busy schedules and heavy workloads but there are some major changes coming that will make our journey a lot easier. Big changes are now being introduced by the Department of Education (DepEd) to make our high school life a more focused, useful and highly relevant bridge to our future dreams.

What is Strengthened SHS?

To ease the load of students, DepEd’s revised K–12 program, dubbed as the Strengthened Senior High School (SHS) Curriculum, cut down the previous 15 core subjects to just five major courses. The Work Immersion time has been extended and the strands are now divided into two distinct pathways: Academic and Technical-Professional (TechPro). The overall aim is to make the final 2 years of secondary education more manageable and useful, to better equip us for college, the work force, or entrepreneurship.

New Subjects / Electives

The new curriculum replaces the 15 heavy core subjects with five integrated learning areas: Effective Communication, General Mathematics, General Science, Life and Career Skills and Kasaysayan at Lipunang Pilipino. Furthermore, the original tracks have been streamlined into two main pathways: the Academic Track and the Technical-Professional (TechPro) Track. In addition, we can choose subjects that align with our goals or interests; you can select electives from different strands.

Work Immersion and Practical Skills

A key highlight of the strengthened program is its heavy focus on real-world skills through expanded Work Immersion. Instead of just sitting through classroom lectures, we get to experience actual workplace training, gain hands-on experience, and earn valuable industry credentials like TESDA certifications. Through stronger partnerships with companies, we get the chance to practice our skills in real work settings, building the confidence and professionalism we need before we even graduate.

Why It Matters to Grade 11 Students

For us Grade 11 students, this means that what we do for the rest of high school is directly catered to what happens after graduation. If you want to go to a university through the competitive entrance exams route, or want to start your own business, or get specialised technical training, or want to get a job immediately after Grade 12, the new curriculum facilitates all the four exit pathways equally. It helps us create a meaningful personal portfolio, master core competencies, and gain confidence without sacrificing our mental health to schoolwork overload.

Conclusion

The Strengthened SHS Curriculum doesn't just reduce the number of subjects but also aids the SHS Students to facilitate and bring out their skills for their future. The future is uncertain but we are more than ready to take on whatever path we choose with a new curriculum designed around our real world success.

GRADE 11 — ONLY THE BEGINNING

By: Rohanna Jurich M. Cabildo
Before Grade 11, I was nervous— not because of the lessons, but because familiar faces were leaving. Some transferred, some chose another school, and only five of us stayed. It was sad, not gonna lie. We used to fight secretly in class, then laugh like nothing happened. Ten–Wakelet, the “tamad” ones— just kidding. But those little moments became memories I didn't want to lose. Then came the question: What do I take in Senior High School? The new curriculum confused me, and I was scared I might get lost, even with only two rooms. So I searched, I thought, and somehow chose STEM— maybe a doctor, maybe a nurse, whatever fits the budget. Then school began. New teachers, new lessons, new introductions. But some classmates were familiar friends from another section, so slowly, I became comfortable, and a little more confident. Then came the election— and somehow, I became Vice President. I wasn't sure if I wanted it. Leadership meant responsibility, and responsibility meant stress. But I stayed. I joined YES-O too, and became Vice President again. Days filled with lessons, recitations I still don't love, dancing, performances, quizzes, activities, and exams waiting around the corner. I even forgot lessons I had already learned before, so maybe Grade 11 wasn't completely new after all. I watched friendships grow, circles form, achievers shine, best friends become closer, and relationships begin. As for me— there was a “thing,” but let's not talk about that. Being an officer wasn't easy. Some wouldn't listen, some wouldn't help, and sometimes leading felt exhausting. Still, I trusted my fellow officers, trusted my classmates, and kept going. And now, Term 1 is almost over. Grade 11–JCF, this is only the beginning. May the days ahead be happier, louder, and brighter— with more memories waiting to be made.

Tulay sa Bukas

By: Maja A. Ramirez
Bagong oras, mas malalim na aral, Sa bagong yugto, tayo ay sumalang. Hindi na bata, hindi pa ganap na matanda, Dito sa Baitang 11, lakas ay binubuo’t sinisikap. Mga numero at agham na pinag-aaralan, Mga salitang nagbibigay ng kaisipan. Kahit mahirap, kahit nakakapagod, Laging may pag-asa, laging may pag-asa’t pag-asa sa puso. Kasama ang kaibigan sa tuwa at hirap, Magtutulungan upang di tayo matuluyang mapagod. Pangarap ay nagliliwanag sa bawat pagsisikap, Para sa kinabukasan, para sa ating layunin. Tulay ito patungo sa ating mga pangarap, Dito nahuhubog ang isip at kalooban. Bawat hakbang ay pag-ahon at pag-asenso, Baitang 11—hahanda tayo sa tagumpay ng buong puso!

Still Climbing: My Grade 11 Journey

By: Zeresh May T. Casauran
I still remember day number one, Walking in scared, no time for fun. New faces everywhere I looked, My hands were shaking, eyes on my desk. Grade 11 feels so big and hard, With endless projects in my yard. Late night study, feeling tired, Just wanting my grades to be inspired. Some days are heavy, I won't lie, There are times I just want to cry. But talking with my trusted friends Helps make the bad feelings end. I take some time to rest and play, To wash the heavy stress away. I’m learning to stand up on my own, And look at how much I have grown. The journey’s not yet at the end, There’s still a long way to ascend. But I will keep on doing my best, Until I finally reach the rest!
